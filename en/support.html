KoePic support

See the User guide for how to use the app.

For questions or bug reports, email us: koepic.support@gmail.com

Please include your phone model (e.g. iPhone 15), OS version, and app version (Settings → About this app in the App).

FAQ

  • Voice sorting does not work: check that KoePic is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition in your phone’s Settings. You can always sort with buttons and typing too.
  • Pro disappeared after changing phones: tap Settings → Restore purchases.
  • How do I cancel? iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Subscriptions.
