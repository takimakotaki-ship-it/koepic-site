Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Introduction

This policy explains how Koepic-Technologies (“we”) handles information in the KoePic smartphone app (“the App”).

We do not collect your photos or your voice

The App keeps your photos, videos, folders and sorting records only on your phone. Nothing is sent to our servers (we do not run any servers).

What the App uses, and why

  • Camera and microphone: to take photos and videos, and to hear folder names you say.
  • Photo library: to save your photos and to sort unsorted photos into folders.
  • Speech recognition: your phone’s built-in speech recognition (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) turns your voice into text. It runs on the phone whenever possible; on some devices or languages Apple or Google may process the audio under their own privacy policies. We never receive your voice.

Ads (free plan)

On the free plan, the App shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising ID (if you allow it), IP address, approximate location, and ad view/tap information to show and measure ads.

On iPhone, we ask for tracking permission before the advertising ID is used. Every feature works even if you decline. In the EEA, the UK and similar regions, we ask for your consent before showing ads, and you can change it in the App’s Settings.

See Google’s policy for details. KoePic Pro shows no ads.

Purchases (KoePic Pro)

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your card details.

We use RevenueCat to check your purchase status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous user ID and purchase records (product, date, etc.). See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Backups

Backup files are saved where you choose (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, your phone, etc.). Automatic backups are saved in the App’s storage on your phone and may be included in your device backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup). We never see your backups.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
